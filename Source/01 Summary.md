# Source Document Summaries

Generated from the documents in `Source/`.

## Overall Tender Context

The source documents relate to TM Technology Services Sdn. Bhd.'s closed RFP `NAE/2026/RFP/01/A1G/BIG` for the **TM & Cradle BIG 2.0 Programme: Design and Development of Predictive and Preventive Platform to Enhance Fibre Infrastructure Resilience**. The proposal set covers submission forms, commercial and financial templates, security obligations, technical requirements, Q&A clarifications, and the draft proof-of-concept/pilot agreement.

Key recurring requirements:

- The Year 1 POC and Pilot must fit within an 8-month window from September 2026 to April 2027.
- Total Year 1 solution cost, inclusive of taxes and all applicable commercial costs, must not exceed RM1.8 million.
- The proposed solution must address fibre disruption risks from rodents/pests/wildlife, theft/vandalism, and construction or third-party damage.
- The platform is expected to provide predictive analytics, risk heatmaps, alerting, workflow orchestration, resource planning, and preventive/mitigation tracking.
- For Year 1, the solution should operate standalone without relying on integration with existing TM systems, while remaining API-ready and portable for future scale-up.
- Submissions must include signed declarations, compliance statements, BOQ, supporting evidence, and confidentiality/integrity commitments.
