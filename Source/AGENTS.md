# AGENTS.md — Source Document Index

Scope: this file applies to the `Source/` directory.

Use this index whenever answering questions, drafting proposal content, checking compliance, or deciding which tender documents to reference. Start with `01 Summary.md` for the overall context, then open the specific source documents listed below as needed.

## Overall Context

- `01 Summary.md` — Human-readable summary of all source documents and key recurring tender requirements. Use this first for orientation, but verify exact wording in the original files before relying on contractual, financial, legal, or submission-critical details.

## Primary Tender Instructions

- `PART I - Instructions to Proposers RFP Falcon (Ver1.0) (1).pdf` — Submission rules, proposal preparation, document set, forms, signatures, checklist usage, price schedule handling, communication, validity, deposit, whistleblowing, and conflict expectations.
- `PART II - Conditions of Tendering Falcon (Ver1.0).pdf` — RFP scope, evaluation phases, SME attendance, pricing conditions, TM rights, eligibility/disqualification rules, taxes, award model, financing, and source-of-supply requirements.
- `Part II - Pre-requisite to RFP.xlsx` — Pre-requisite compliance response. Use for local-presence compliance in Malaysia and related sign-off fields.

## Submission Forms and Declarations

- `01 PART III - Appendix A_Checklist of Tender Submission_amend.docx` — Submission checklist covering Volume 1 Summary Proposal, Volume 2 Commercial Proposal, and Volume 3 Technical Proposal. Use to confirm required attachments and submission completeness.
- `02 PART III - Appendix B_Form of RFP (BIG).docx` — Formal offer letter / Form of RFP. Use for proposer offer wording, proposal validity, pricing summary, agreement period, guarantees, tax-inclusive pricing, and signatory details.
- `03 PART III - Appendix F1 - Company Profiles.docx` — Company profile template. Use for Krenovator company background, registration, directors, shareholders, staffing, services, commercial experience, financial-document requirements, and BCM profile.
- `04 PART III - Appendix F3 - Summary of Tenderer's Financial Strength.xlsx` — Financial strength workbook. Use for audited-account fields, financial ratios, and supporting finance evidence requirements.
- `05 PART III - Appendix G_Non Disclosure Agreement.docx` — NDA. Use for confidentiality obligations, permitted disclosure, employee/representative obligations, personal data handling, and protection of RFP information.
- `06 PART III - Appendix H_Declaration of Independency & RPT.docx` — Independence, conflict, sanctions, legal standing, blacklisting, bankruptcy, integrity, auditor affiliation, and related-party transaction declaration.
- `KIV_07 PART III - Appendix I_Principal's Warranty (BIG).docx` — Principal warranty template, marked KIV. Use only if a principal/manufacturer warranty is needed for hardware, software, licenses, or supplied items.
- `08 PART III - Appendix J_LOU (BIG).docx` — Letter of Undertaking template for supplier/principal/subcontractor support, including takeover obligations if the tenderer defaults.
- `09 PART III - Appendix K_OSH Mgt. System Questionnaire (BIG).docx` — OSH and environmental management questionnaire. Use for safety policy, certifications, procedures, incident controls, training, inspections, hazard reporting, and references.
- `10 PART III - Appendix L_Integrity Pledge_BIG.pdf` — Integrity pledge. Use for anti-bribery, anti-corruption, transparency, confidentiality, conflict disclosure, audit cooperation, whistleblowing, and Malaysian-law obligations.

## Contract and Legal Terms

- `Appendix D1 to Contract T&Cs.pdf` — Statement of compliance template for full compliance with contract terms. Use when no contractual exceptions are being proposed.
- `Appendix D2_Statement of non-compliance to TnC.xlsx` — Non-compliance template for contract terms. Use only when proposing exceptions or amendments; note that the extracted tender reference may be inconsistent and should be verified before use.
- `Falcon_Innovation POC  Pilot Agreement.pdf` — Draft Innovation POC and Pilot Agreement. Use for stage-gate structure, POC/Pilot obligations, deliverables, payment, termination, liability, insurance, OSH, indemnity, IP, confidentiality, data protection, anti-corruption, audit, DR/BCP, and dispute clauses.

## Technical, Security, and Commercial Requirements

- `BIG Programme_SOC.docx` — Main technical specifications and statement of compliance. Use as the primary source for solution objectives, required capabilities, architecture, data strategy, AI methodology, integration approach, scalability, security, POC plan, Gantt, milestones, RACI, KPIs, staffing, evidence, declarations, and the RM1.8 million Year 1 cap.
- `BIG Programme_SOC Addendum.docx` — Technical addendum on startup team and personnel availability. Use for key-personnel commitment, replacement approval, equivalent-or-better replacement requirements, and schedule/quality continuity.
- `BIG 2.0 Programme_FALCON BOQ.xlsx` — BOQ and commercial pricing workbook. Use for Year 1 POC/Pilot pricing, cost categories, payment milestones, rate card, commercial compliance, and the total cost cap through 30 April 2027.
- `Reference Baseline for Solution Design and BOQ.pptx` — One-slide reference baseline. Use for solution-design and BOQ alignment, especially when checking whether proposed architecture and cost items map to TM’s expected baseline.
- `Appendix B.1 Information Security Annex.pdf` — Contractual information-security annex. Use for supplier security controls across standards compliance, incident management, cryptography, privacy by design, vulnerability fixing, hardening, application security, endpoint/network/physical security, audit, operations, data handling, backup, and BCP.
- `Appendix B.2 Vendor Security Hygiene.pdf` — Vendor security hygiene requirements. Use for security policy, identity/access control, password and leaver controls, device restrictions, least privilege, encryption, retention/destruction, anti-malware, vulnerability remediation, configuration, logging, secure development, training, and incident response.

## Clarifications and Q&A

- `Batch1 Q&A_BIG2.0 RFP (1).pdf` — First clarification set. Use for eligibility, financial evidence alternatives, Year 1 standalone operation, data availability after award, future API preferences, external data sourcing, prototype/simulation evidence, subcontracting, NDA submission, and near-real-time workflow targets of 15 minutes for POC and 10 minutes for Pilot.
- `Batch2 Q&A_BIG2.0 RFP.pdf` — Second clarification set. Use for fibre cable/core-count scope, DAS/existing TM asset constraints, delayed SSM documents, pricing/monitoring addendum expectation, and SME/local startup partner accountability.

- For tender compliance questions, consult `PART I - Instructions to Proposers RFP Falcon (Ver1.0) (1).pdf`, `PART II - Conditions of Tendering Falcon (Ver1.0).pdf`, `01 PART III - Appendix A_Checklist of Tender Submission_amend.docx`, and the relevant appendix.
- For technical solution questions, consult `BIG Programme_SOC.docx`, `BIG Programme_SOC Addendum.docx`, `Reference Baseline for Solution Design and BOQ.pptx`, both Q&A PDFs, and the security annexes.
- For pricing or commercial questions, consult `BIG 2.0 Programme_FALCON BOQ.xlsx`, `02 PART III - Appendix B_Form of RFP (BIG).docx`, `PART II - Conditions of Tendering Falcon (Ver1.0).pdf`, and the Q&A PDFs.
- For contract/legal obligations, consult `Falcon_Innovation POC  Pilot Agreement.pdf`, `Appendix D1 to Contract T&Cs.pdf`, `Appendix D2_Statement of non-compliance to TnC.xlsx`, `05 PART III - Appendix G_Non Disclosure Agreement.docx`, and `10 PART III - Appendix L_Integrity Pledge_BIG.pdf`.
- For company, financial, and eligibility content, consult `03 PART III - Appendix F1 - Company Profiles.docx`, `04 PART III - Appendix F3 - Summary of Tenderer's Financial Strength.xlsx`, `06 PART III - Appendix H_Declaration of Independency & RPT.docx`, and `Part II - Pre-requisite to RFP.xlsx`.
- For supplier, principal, subcontractor, OSH, and warranty obligations, consult `08 PART III - Appendix J_LOU (BIG).docx`, `KIV_07 PART III - Appendix I_Principal's Warranty (BIG).docx`, and `09 PART III - Appendix K_OSH Mgt. System Questionnaire (BIG).docx`.
