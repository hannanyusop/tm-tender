## What This Project Is About

This repository supports the tender response for TM Technology Services Sdn. Bhd.'s closed RFP for the TM & Cradle BIG 2.0 Programme. The RFP seeks a predictive and preventive platform that improves fibre infrastructure resilience by helping TM predict, detect, assess, and mitigate disruption risks before they become service-impacting incidents.

The target disruption risks include:

- Rodents, pests, and wildlife affecting fibre infrastructure.
- Theft, vandalism, or deliberate damage.
- Construction activity and other third-party damage near fibre assets.

The proposed solution is expected to support predictive analytics, risk heatmaps, near-real-time alerts, workflow orchestration, mitigation tracking, dashboard reporting, and intelligent resource planning.

## Tender Response Scope

The workspace is organized to help prepare a complete tender submission across three Phase 1 proposal volumes:

| Volume | Focus | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume I | Summary of Proposal | Covering letter, checklist, Form of RFP, prerequisite response. |
| Volume II | Commercial Proposal | Contract compliance, company profile, financial strength, SSM evidence, NDA, declarations, OSH, integrity pledge. |
| Volume III | Technical Proposal | Technical SOC, security annex, technical addendum, unpriced BOQ, architecture and supporting evidence. |

Phase 2 is separate and should contain the final priced submission only when TM officially requests it.

## Key Constraints

| Area | Requirement / Constraint |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery window | Year 1 POC and Pilot are expected within an 8-month period from September 2026 to April 2027. |
| Budget | Total Year 1 POC and Pilot cost must not exceed RM1.8 million, inclusive of taxes and applicable commercial costs. |
| System dependency | Year 1 solution should operate standalone without depending on existing TM systems, while remaining API-ready for future scale-up. |
| Submission separation | Technical submission must exclude priced BOQ or priced schedule content. |
| Compliance | Submission must include required declarations, evidence, signed forms, and source-specific compliance documents. |
