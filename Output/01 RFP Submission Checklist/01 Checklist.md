## Final Sign-Off

### Proposer Confirmation

- [ ] Confirm all documents above have been submitted by the RFP closing date.
- [ ] Confirm the proposal and all exhibits, schedules, documents, and instruments are true, accurate, and complete.
- [ ] Confirm the proposal contains all information necessary to avoid misleading TM on any material fact.
- [ ] Add authorized proposer signature.
- [ ] Add authorized signatory name.
- [ ] Add authorized signatory designation.
- [ ] Add company stamp, if required.

### TM Confirmation

- [ ] Reserve section for TM signature.
- [ ] Reserve section for TM representative name.
- [ ] Reserve section for TM representative designation.

## Submission Quality Checks

- [ ] All required appendices are complete and internally consistent.
- [ ] All signatures, dates, names, designations, and company stamps are present where required.
- [ ] Proposal validity, pricing, commercial terms, and agreement period match the Form of RFP.
- [ ] Commercial BOQ includes prices only in the commercial proposal.
- [ ] Technical BOQ copy excludes prices.
- [ ] All financial evidence matches the years and alternatives accepted under the RFP clarifications.
- [ ] All applicable supplier, principal, subcontractor, NDA, warranty, and undertaking documents are included.
- [ ] All technical evidence supports the Part V SOC requirements.
- [ ] Information security submissions cover Appendix B.1 and vendor security hygiene expectations.
- [ ] Final package filenames are clear, numbered, and mapped to the three proposal volumes.
