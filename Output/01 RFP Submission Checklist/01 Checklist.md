# RFP Submission Checklist

Tender reference: `NAE/2026/RFP/01/A1G/BIG`  
Source: `Source/01 PART III - Appendix A_Checklist of Tender Submission_amend.docx`

## Use Instructions

- Tick each item once the document is completed, signed or stamped where required, and included in the final submission pack.
- Keep final signed copies separate from working drafts.
- Verify all appendix numbering against the final tender pack before submission.
- Items marked `IF APPLICABLE` should be either included or clearly marked not applicable with supporting rationale.
- Phase 1 submission must be a virus-free softcopy proposal uploaded into three separate folders: Volume I, Volume II, and Volume III.
- Phase 2 priced submission is separate and should be submitted only when officially requested by TM.

## Portal and Packaging Checks

- [ ] Upload Phase 1 documents into the correct designated folders: Volume I, Volume II, and Volume III.
- [ ] Confirm all softcopy files are virus-free.
- [ ] Confirm all submitted files are readable, uncorrupted, and error-free.
- [ ] Apply electronic or digital signatures to every document where required.
- [ ] If hardcopy submission is required, bind documents securely and mark packages with the RFP reference and RFP description.
- [ ] Do not include any price list, priced BOQ, or priced schedule in the Technical Proposal package.

## Final Sign-Off

### Proposer Confirmation

- [ ] Confirm all documents above have been submitted by the RFP closing date.
- [ ] Confirm the proposal and all exhibits, schedules, documents, and instruments are true, accurate, and complete.
- [ ] Confirm the proposal contains all information necessary to avoid misleading TM on any material fact.
- [ ] Add authorized proposer signature.
- [ ] Add authorized signatory name.
- [ ] Add authorized signatory designation.
- [ ] Add company stamp, if required.

### TM Confirmation

- [ ] Reserve section for TM signature.
- [ ] Reserve section for TM representative name.
- [ ] Reserve section for TM representative designation.

## Submission Quality Checks

- [ ] All required appendices are complete and internally consistent.
- [ ] All signatures, dates, names, designations, and company stamps are present where required.
- [ ] Proposal validity, pricing, commercial terms, and agreement period match the Form of RFP.
- [ ] Price Schedule total is inserted in the Form of RFP under RFP Value.
- [ ] All prices are quoted in Ringgit Malaysia and inclusive of applicable taxes or charges.
- [ ] Commercial BOQ includes prices only in the commercial proposal.
- [ ] Technical BOQ copy excludes prices.
- [ ] All financial evidence matches the years and alternatives accepted under the RFP clarifications.
- [ ] All applicable supplier, principal, subcontractor, NDA, warranty, and undertaking documents are included.
- [ ] If equipment, items, or components are offered, state country of origin and principal's registered name and address.
- [ ] If financing is proposed, include facility type, currency, repayment currency, offer expiry date, and any guarantee required.
- [ ] All technical evidence supports the Part V SOC requirements.
- [ ] Information security submissions cover Appendix B.1 and vendor security hygiene expectations.
- [ ] Final package filenames are clear, numbered, and mapped to the three proposal volumes.
